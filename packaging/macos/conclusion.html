Set up your call audio

Restart your Mac if prompted, then open OpenKrisp from Applications and allow microphone access.

In your calling app, select OpenKrisp Microphone as the microphone and OpenKrisp Speaker as the speaker, using stereo channels 1–2.

In OpenKrisp, send your physical microphone to OpenKrisp Microphone. Start Incoming audio from OpenKrisp Speaker and select your physical headphones or speakers as output.

Enable incoming speech noise reduction for calls; keep music in bypass. For speakers, check that echo cancellation is active. Headphones remain the fallback.

Select these devices in both apps and save your updated OpenKrisp profiles.

If either OpenKrisp device is missing, rerun this installer and restart when prompted.
