Finish setting up your call audio

Restart your Mac if prompted, then open OpenKrisp from Applications. Allow microphone access, choose your physical microphone, and start Virtual microphone.

In your calling app, select BlackHole 2ch as the microphone. Select BlackHole 16ch as the speaker, using stereo channels 1–2.

In OpenKrisp, start Incoming audio from BlackHole 16ch and choose your physical headphones or speakers as its output. For speakers, check that echo cancellation is active. Headphones remain the fallback while speakerphone acceptance testing is pending.

If BlackHole is missing, rerun this installer and restart your Mac.
