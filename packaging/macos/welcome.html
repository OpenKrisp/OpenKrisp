OpenKrisp

This installer adds OpenKrisp to Applications and installs BlackHole 2ch for your cleaned microphone and BlackHole 16ch for incoming call audio.

Each existing BlackHole installation of the same or a newer version is reused independently. BlackHole is shared with other audio apps and stays installed if you delete OpenKrisp.

Quit OpenKrisp and other audio apps before continuing. Installing either audio driver requires an administrator password and a restart.
