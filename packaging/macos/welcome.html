OpenKrisp

This installer adds OpenKrisp to Applications and installs BlackHole 2ch, the open-source audio driver that sends your cleaned microphone to other apps.

An existing BlackHole 2ch installation of the same or a newer version is reused. BlackHole is shared with other audio apps and stays installed if you delete OpenKrisp.

Quit OpenKrisp and other audio apps before continuing. Installing the audio driver requires an administrator password and a restart.
