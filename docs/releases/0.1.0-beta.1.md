# OpenKrisp 0.1.0 beta 1 — Apple Silicon

OpenKrisp reduces background noise in microphone and incoming call audio locally
on your Mac. This experimental beta includes the desktop application and the
OpenKrisp Microphone and OpenKrisp Speaker virtual audio devices.

## Download and install

Choose the Apple Silicon `.pkg` in the release assets. This build targets M-series
Macs with macOS 11.0 or later; compatibility across that full OS range is not yet
qualified. Intel and Linux binaries are not included in this release.

**This beta is not notarized by Apple.** The app and audio drivers use ad-hoc
signatures and the installer package is unsigned. macOS may block the installer
or app on first launch. For a download you trust, Apple's per-app **Privacy &
Security → Open Anyway** flow may be available. Managed Macs may disallow it.
See the included installation guide and
[Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

Close audio apps before installation, authorize the installer, and restart if
prompted. Open OpenKrisp, grant microphone access, and select your physical
microphone and headphones. In your call app, choose **OpenKrisp Microphone** as
input and **OpenKrisp Speaker** as output, then start the matching OpenKrisp routes.
Headphones are recommended for this beta.

## Beta limitations

- Clean-Mac browser-download and first-launch behavior, installed-driver upgrade
  and restart, and the new named drivers in real calls still need broader testing.
- Speakerphone echo cancellation, device transitions, and long-running audio
  sessions are not fully qualified.
- Updates are manual. Quit OpenKrisp and audio apps before running a newer installer.
- Deleting the app does not uninstall its audio drivers; follow the included guide.

OpenKrisp's original code is source available under PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.0.
Third-party components retain their own licenses. The release includes a source
snapshot and checksums; SHA-256 checksums detect changed downloads but do not
replace an Apple signature.
